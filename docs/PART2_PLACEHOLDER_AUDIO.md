# Part 2: placeholder audio and dry-run pipeline

Part 1 was merged and its signed 0.1.233 APK, min-HP CSV and logs delivered before this work began. This layer must not change its gameplay tuning, damage, loot RNG, anti-camp or objective rules. No real sound candidates have been selected or promoted. docs/SOUND_SPEC.md is the unchanged user attachment.

The SoundManager autoload creates Master/Music/SFX/UI buses, Music low-pass and -1 dB SFX/Master hard limiters. Twenty-four 2D voices grow to thirty with two reserved music voices, hard total32. Metadata controls per-ID cooldown, simultaneous instances, priority, variation and non-repeating variants. Protected cues wait/coalesce when no safe voice can be stolen. Audio has an independent RNG. Settings persist in user://audio_settings.cfg; SOUND is available from title and pause, with Credits and Vibration. App background notifications suspend every voice and suppress new playback.

All66 SFX cue IDs in section5 have synthetic placeholders. Nine original jsfxr-compatible oscillator/envelope presets produce36 cached 44.1kHz mono WAV resources; cue-specific pitch/mix settings differentiate them. This is an in-house subset, not an assertion that unmodified upstream jsfxr produced these sounds. CREDITS.md records every virtual resource. A checked-in in-house Ogg pulse supplies a deliberately simple placeholder riff for the five music roles; it is not final-length final-quality music. No ffmpeg or extra Python package is required at APK startup. SFX are preloaded before gameplay; Ogg music uses AudioStreamOggVorbis. All currently implemented weapon/reward/objective/special-enemy events are wired; Trucker cues remain callable catalog hooks for the future boss stage, not invented boss gameplay.

Music follows breather/announce/event/surge/level-up/pause/low-HP phases and ducks for announcements/reveals. XP has the0.3s semitone climb. Non-vocal impacts/deaths remain quiet; shamblers have no ambient vocal cue and footsteps are absent. Sound-off remains a gameplay-independent mix change. Phone speaker audibility, 60FPS and ten-minute annoyance acceptance cannot be established by headless tests or offline desktop recording.

The separate audio_pipeline catalog contains66SFX+5music pieces. Its generation graphs are API adaptations of the official Comfy Stable Audio3 family workflow with Small checkpoints; actual execution on the owner's local installation remains untested. Default find/generate/promote modes are non-writing dry runs. Review selection and processed-preview approval are distinct. No real res://audio promotion or PC installations were performed. Setup, licensing conditions, known PC paths and exact PowerShell commands are in audio_pipeline/README.md.

Validation:343 audio assertions plus100 tuning and234 movement assertions; existing regression suites retained. Pipeline tests use only temporary synthetic fixtures, never real library/model generation or promotion. Final build/recording receipt is recorded on the PR after retrieval and checksum verification.
